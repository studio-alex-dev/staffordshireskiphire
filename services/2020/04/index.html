Keep Construction Sites Clean with Skip Hire

30/04/2020/in News /by Jon Price

Large and small scale domestic and commercial construction can quickly accumulate large amounts of waste and rubbish.

Before you can consider opening a building, the site will need to be cleared of any debris, extra materials and dirt – with the most efficient method being skip hire from Staffordshire Skip Hire.

For our latest post, we are going to share some advice on how to keep your site clean and tidy, no matter the size of the project.

Six Tasks when Cleaning Construction Areas

Before your work is complete on a project, you will need to make sure your site is clean and waste-free, which is doubly important if this is your own home.

Here are the essentials to efficient construction site cleaning from our team –

  1. Book Your Skip Hire – Before starting, ensure you have given Staffordshire Skip Hire a call so your waste can be disposed of properly.
  2. Identify Recyclable Items – Identify what can be recycled before you start your clear out. Materials such as wood and metal can be repurposed plus glass, making your project more environmentally friendly. As you are dealing with construction waste or business rubbish you have a duty of care to make sure it is disposed of properly.
  3. Organise and Separate Waste – When you have split up what can and cannot be recycled, it is a good idea to organise separate areas for rubbish to be collected. Naturally, you will want to put waste into a skip, which helps to keep sites neat and today.
  4. Separate Larger Items – Ensure larger items that require specialist removal or equipment because of their size and weight, are kept away from general waste. This ensures there is plenty of room for both piles to be removed without disruption. Read the certain restrictions on what can be placed in skips.
  5. Arrange a Skip Collection Date – Now it is time to arrange a suitable date for the collection of your skip. This part of the process can be done during the initial hiring stage, which gives you a timetable to work towards too.
  6. Have One Final Clean – Once your waste has been taken away, it is time for a final cleaning session with the mops and brooms. This includes your building’s interiors, plus the windows, removing any window stickers plus you can take away any protective covers from glass, equipment and floors.

Staffordshire Skip Hire have been providing the most cost-effective and reliable waste removal service for customers across Stoke on Trent for a number of years.

Working with us will keep your project on time and on budget, and you can be assured your rubbish will be disposed responsibly and in an environmentally friendly and legal manner.

Effective Office Clearances using Skip Hire

15/04/2020/in News /by Jon Price

Clearing out an office can be a daunting task, especially if you are short on time and without a workstation but fear not Staffordshire Skip Hire are here to help!

If you are making a long-awaited move from a dusty old office to a brand new modern one, you can read our easy-to-follow office clearance guide below.

5 Questions You Need to Ask About Office Clearance

Our team of specialists often get asked these five questions relating to office clearances –

How much time do I have?

It is crucial to consider how much time you have to complete your office clearance. Planning is key, as not knowing how long it could take could leave you rushing and making mistakes. This could mean throwing away items you would have kept because you were in a rush.
It is always best to clarify if the clearance will be a gradual process or if the waste accumulated needs removed immediately.

What waste are you getting rid of?

When you start clearing out your office you will stumble across different types of waste that need removing. This could be old stationery, furniture or computers which need disposing of sustainably and efficiently.
We would recommend separating your waste so you can identify those bulkier items which require removal, plus this will give you a better idea of what service you need.

How much waste do you have?

Now you have clarified what you are getting rid of, it is time to get a rough idea of how much there is to clear. The amount of waste will have a direct impact on the cost of your clearance service. Failure to clarify this could cause issues such as ordering the wrong sized skip. On the other side of the spectrum, ordering a skip too small could see waste overflowing. Checking how much waste you have will definitely save you money when you need skip hire.

What service do I need?

Now it is time to choose which service best suits your office clearance project.

Skip hire is our bread and butter at Staffordshire Skip Hire, which offers a longer-term approach for office clearances. Our skips can be hired for short spaces of time (an initial 2 weeks) or longer-term if your project is more extensive. Skips come in various shapes and sizes, ranging from 2-tonne mini skips to 12 cubic yard lidded skips.

How Do I Book a Skip Hire?

Hopefully, we have given you food for thought and you have decided what skip will be best for your office clearance.

Our skips can be booked online and paid for using Paypal or Credit Card.

Get in Touch for More Information

If you have any questions about skip hire relating to office clearances or any other waste removal projects, please contact us today.

Skip Hire Makes Shed Clearouts Simple

01/04/2020/in News /by Jon Price

If clearing out your shed is now at the top of your domestic jobs list; it will be made much easier by hiring a skip from Staffordshire Skip Hire.

You will save yourself a lot of trips to your local recycling centre plus you can keep your home clear of unwanted tools and waste with a perfect-sized skip.

Summer is getting closer all the time, and if you are looking to start work on a new garden project, having a good clearout will help free up plenty of space for a base of operations.

5 Essential Tips for Your Shed Clearout

Choose a Nice Day to Begin – Check the weather forecast a few days before planning your clearout, as if the heavens start to open halfway through your task becomes more difficult. If there is not a day without rain in the near future, look at laying a waterproof sheet over your work area so you can quickly wrap up items when it starts to rain.

Create a Clearout Strategy – It is tempting to dive straight in and get started, but having a plan in advance makes your task more efficient. Start at the front of your shed and work your way towards the back is the smartest solution, particularly if you have your hired skip ready to go. This method helps to keep you focused plus you will be able to see your progress as or when your dedication starts to waver.

Be Ruthless – It can be difficult but you need to be somewhat ruthless when it comes to a clearout of any kind. If you are simply moving some items around in the shed, then you are not having a clearout and your hired skip is going to waste.

Keep It Clean – Once you have cleared out your shed, giving it a good clean should be your first task. Clearouts typically happen every couple of years, so making it as pleasant as possible is definitely a worthwhile task and will really highlight your achievements.

A Lick of Paint – Once the inside of your shed is suitably clean, why not look at making the exterior look even better? A new coat of varnish or paint will refresh the look of your shed and make plenty of difference to the overall appearance of your garden.

Staffordshire Skip Hire would recommend maintaining the cleanliness of your shed as long as you can, as this will mean the next day you spend clearing it out again will be a long time in the future. Remember, without one of our skips for your waste, you could end up with a simple reshuffle rather than a thorough clearout.
